## Create your first Klex Bot

The default — and recommended — way to run a Klex Bot is hosted in the [Klex Cloud](https://cloud.klex.bot): nothing to install, no machine of your own involved. If you have spare hardware or want full control over where your Klex Bot lives, you can host it yourself instead — you choose this during setup.

<Steps>
<Step title="Sign up to the Klex Cloud">
Go to the [Klex Cloud](https://cloud.klex.bot) and sign up. In the left sidebar, open the Bots panel and click on `Create new Klex Bot`.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose hosting">
Select where your Klex Bot should run. **Cloud hosting** is the recommended default — the Klex Cloud runs the Klex Bot for you. **Self-hosting** runs it on your own machine; the portal explains the required steps once you select it.
</Step>
<Step title="Set name and style">
Enter the name your Klex Bot should use — it will know that this is its own name — and pick its style.
</Step>
<Step title="Define the soul">
Describe the personality and the job of your Klex Bot. You have two options: [Creator mode](/bot/concepts/soul) (*recommended!*) and [God message mode](/bot/concepts/god-messages).

[Learn more about the soul](/bot/concepts/soul).
</Step>
<Step title="Connect providers and select models">
Connect the model providers you want to use and select the models your Klex Bot should run on.
<Panel title="Good to know">
Provider credentials are stored in the Klex Bot's own config, inside its hosted environment — **not** centrally in the cloud.

Klex supports a variety of subscriptions, first-party providers, local LLM providers, and generic API providers. Learn more about [model providers and model selection](/bot/concepts/models).
</Panel>
</Step>
<Step title="Connect tools and integrations">
Connect the tools and platforms your Klex Bot should be active on. The **Klex Cloud** helps you easily create dedicated accounts for the Klex Bot in each integrated app, and you can of course also connect any other MCP.
</Step>
</Steps>

That's it — your first Klex Bot is live.

<Panel title="Good to know">
A Klex Bot has no automatic access to your files, machines, or accounts. Everything it can reach outside its own brain — computers, channels, apps — is connected explicitly via [MCP](/bot/concepts/mcp).
</Panel>

## Self-hosting

If you have spare hardware or want full control over where your Klex Bot lives, host it yourself. Select self-hosting during setup and follow the steps in the portal. To install Klex on your machine, run:

<Tabs param="install">
  <Tab title="macOS">`curl -fsSL https://klex.bot/install.sh | sh`</Tab>
  <Tab title="Linux">`curl -fsSL https://klex.bot/install.sh | sh`</Tab>
  <Tab title="Windows">`irm https://klex.bot/install.ps1 | iex`</Tab>
  <Tab title="WSL">`curl -fsSL https://klex.bot/install.sh | sh`</Tab>
</Tabs>
