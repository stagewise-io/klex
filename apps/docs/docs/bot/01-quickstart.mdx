Create a Klex Bot to work alongside you. Klex recommends Cloud hosting for most users; you can also run a Bot on your own computer.

## Cloud-hosted setup

1. Sign in to [Klex Cloud](https://cloud.klex.bot) and click **Create a new Bot**.
2. Give your Bot a job, or choose a suggested one, and customize its appearance.
3. Click **Create Bot in the Klex Cloud**.
4. Configure its character traits. Start with the suggested defaults or change them to make the Bot behave the way you want.
5. Choose the models the Bot should use, or import model configuration from another Bot in your Cloud account.
6. Your Bot starts and sends you a message. Reply to get started.

You can connect the apps and computers your Bot needs for its work. See [Connections](/bot/concepts/brain-and-computers).

</Tab>

## Self-hosted setup

1. Sign in to [Klex Cloud](https://cloud.klex.bot), click **Create a new Bot**, and choose its job and appearance.
2. Choose the self-hosted option and follow the setup flow. Copy the complete command provided by Klex Cloud, paste it into a terminal on the computer where the Bot should run, and execute it.
3. Follow the setup instructions to configure the Bot's character and models.

Keep the Bot running on your computer so it can work. To restart it, run the following command in a terminal and select your Bot:

```sh
klex
```

### Run without Cloud

Cloud enrollment is optional. To configure a Bot directly in the Klex app, follow the [standalone setup guide](/bot/guides/standalone-setup).
