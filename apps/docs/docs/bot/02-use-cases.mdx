A Klex Bot can help with work that involves information and actions in connected apps or computers. Start with a specific task and give the Bot access only to what it needs.

These are examples to try, not a list of guaranteed integrations. Each workflow depends on the capabilities of its MCP connections and the permissions of its accounts.

## Summarize project information

Ask the Bot to summarize open issues, recent updates, or project documents for your team. Connect the source of the information and, if you want the summary posted, the destination messaging app.

For example: “Summarize this week’s open issues in the project and post the key risks in our project channel.” Check that the Bot can read the intended project and post in that channel.

## Prepare a support response

Ask the Bot to draft an answer using approved support material. Connect the relevant knowledge source and review the draft before sending it. Avoid granting access to customer records or credentials the Bot does not need. See [Protect secrets in support Bots](/guides/support-bot-secrets).

## Coordinate recurring work

Ask the Bot to prepare a regular status update or review a recurring checklist. Connect the information source, the destination for results, and a scheduling service that supports the required schedule and event delivery. See [Automate recurring tasks](/bot/guides/recurring-tasks).

## Start safely

1. [Create a Bot](/bot/quickstart) and describe its role.
2. [Connect the apps or computers](/bot/concepts/brain-and-computers) needed for the task and scope its account permissions.
3. Test with non-sensitive data and review the result. For high-impact work, use an independent review process before applying changes. See [Use Klex Bots securely](/guides/secure-bot-usage).
