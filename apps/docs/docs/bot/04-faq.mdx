Find answers about getting started, hosting, connections, and operating Klex Bots.

## Getting started

- **How do Klex Bots work?** A Bot runs in its own environment and works in computers and apps connected through MCP. See [How Klex Bots work](/bot/concepts/how-bots-work).
- **What can I use a Bot for?** A Bot can work with people and other agents in connected apps and computers. What it can do depends on its connections and account permissions. See [How Klex Bots work](/bot/concepts/how-bots-work).
- **How do I move a workflow from another agent?** Gather its knowledge, routines, and instructions in a handover document, then use them to set up your Klex Bot. See [Migrate from another agent](/bot/migrate-from-another-agent).
- **Can I use a Bot for business work?** Yes. Give it a job and connect the apps and computers it needs. See [Critical operations](/guides/trust-critical-operations).
- **Can I speak with an existing customer?** Contact [Klex support](mailto:support@klex.bot) to ask about customer references.

## How Klex Bots work

- **How does a Bot remember and learn?** A Bot uses learned and pre-configured skills, together with factual knowledge from past work. These help the Bot carry out its job and recall useful information about its environment. See [Klex Bot Memory](/bot/concepts/memory).
- **How is a Bot’s environment different from a connected computer?** Its own environment keeps it running and manages memory; connected computers and apps are where it works. See [Connections](/bot/concepts/brain-and-computers).
- **Which model providers can I use?** Klex supports a range, including OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, local Ollama, and compatible custom endpoints. Available models depend on the provider and your account. See [Models](/bot/concepts/models).
- **Can a Bot use MCP servers and add them itself?** Yes. A Bot can add a new remote HTTP or HTTPS server, but cannot edit or remove existing server configurations through that capability. See [MCP and Klex Bots](/bot/concepts/mcp).
- **Can a Bot use Agent Skills?** Yes. Skills give the Bot reusable instructions for tasks, so it can apply useful procedures as it works. See [Agent Skills](/bot/concepts/agent-skills).
- **How do Bots communicate with people and other Bots?** People can chat with a Bot in the Cloud dashboard. Bots can also communicate with people and other Bots through connected apps with the necessary messaging capabilities and account access. See [Communication](/bot/concepts/communication).

## Operating Klex Bots

- **How can I review a Bot’s activity?** For a Cloud-hosted or enrolled Bot, use the Cloud dashboard to see activity and chat. A self-hosted Bot also has local diagnostics. See [Review activity](/bot/guides/observability).
- **Can I chat with a Bot on multiple channels?** Yes, with suitable messaging connections and account access to each conversation. See [Chat on multiple channels](/bot/guides/multi-channel-chat).
- **Can a Bot handle multiple requests?** A Bot can receive multiple requests. You can also divide independent work among multiple Bots. See [Parallelize work](/bot/guides/parallelize).
- **How do I set up recurring tasks?** Connect a scheduling service and tell the Bot what to do, when to do it, and where to send the result. See [Automate recurring tasks](/bot/guides/recurring-tasks).
- **How do I stop a Bot and manage its access?** Stop a Cloud-hosted Bot from the dashboard, or close the self-hosted app. Manage connected accounts’ permissions in their services. See [Control and limits](/bot/guides/control-and-limits) for headless shutdown and other controls.
- **What are the Bot and connection limits?** Contact [Klex support](mailto:support@klex.bot) for current limits.
- **Can Klex Bots work with other agents?** They can coordinate through a shared service when both have the necessary access and capabilities. See [Work with other agents](/bot/guides/interoperability).

## Reliability

- **Can I monitor a self-hosted Bot’s health?** Yes. Enable the optional health listener to monitor the Klex process and its readiness. See [Monitor a self-hosted Bot](/bot/reliability/availability#monitor-a-self-hosted-bot).
- **Can a Bot work around the clock?** Yes. Keep its hosting environment running and its model and connections available for the work you want it to do. See [Availability](/bot/reliability/availability).
- **Do I have to re-create a Bot after an update?** Generally, no. Your Bot keeps its configuration, chats, and memory in its data directory. Check the release notes when updating. See [Updates](/bot/reliability/updates).

## Cloud and hosting

- **Can I run a Bot without Klex Cloud?** Yes. Cloud enrollment is not required for a self-hosted Bot, though the standalone experience is less complete. See [Hosted vs. self-hosted](/cloud/hosted-vs-local).
- **Who can access a Bot’s memory?** Administrators can access it through the Bot’s Admin API. For Cloud-hosted Bots, limited Klex staff can access chats or memory for agreed support debugging; access is logged and visible to you. Cloud-hosted data is encrypted in transit and at rest. See [Data privacy](/cloud/data-privacy) for the scope and applicable terms.
- **Can I turn off analytics for a self-hosted Bot?** Yes. Use `--no-analytics`, `KLEX_NO_ANALYTICS=1`, or `DO_NOT_TRACK=1`. The explicit `--analytics` flag overrides the environment opt-outs. Operational telemetry is separate and has its own controls. These settings do not disable connections to providers, MCP servers, or Cloud. See [Analytics and telemetry](/cloud/data-privacy#analytics-and-telemetry-for-a-self-hosted-bot).
- **Can I self-host Klex Cloud?** Self-hosted Klex Cloud is available to Enterprise customers. This is separate from self-hosting an individual Bot. See [Self-hosting the Cloud](/cloud/self-hosting).

## Klex Bots in your company

- **How do I manage a Bot’s permissions in an app?** Configure its account’s access in that app, granting only what it needs. See [Connections to computers and apps](/bot/concepts/brain-and-computers).
- **How do I reduce unauthorized access and information disclosure?** Restrict who can reach the Bot and give its accounts access only to the information they need. See [Prevent information theft](/guides/prevent-information-theft).
- **How do I reduce the risk of a support Bot leaking secrets?** Avoid granting access to unnecessary credentials or sensitive data, scope its accounts, and test with non-sensitive data. See [Protect secrets in support Bots](/guides/support-bot-secrets).
- **How do I limit the impact of a compromised Bot?** Restrict its account permissions and connected environments, and use independent approval for high-impact changes. See [Limit blast radius](/guides/limit-blast-radius).
- **How can I use a Bot for critical work?** Limit access and use an independent review or approval process for high-impact work. Klex does not currently enforce action-level approvals. See [Critical operations](/guides/trust-critical-operations).
- **How can Klex Bots work together?** Bots can coordinate in a shared conversation. Give each Bot access and a clear role, then use the conversation for handoffs. See [Make Klex Bots work together](/guides/bot-collaboration).
