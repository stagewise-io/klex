> **How does a Klex Bot know who it is — and which exact person is talking to it?**

Identity has two sides for a Klex Bot: who the Klex Bot itself is, and who it is talking to. Who the Klex Bot is lives in its [soul](/bot/concepts/soul) — a character description that only the admin can change through god messages. Who it is talking to is something the Klex Bot keeps track of itself: it maps every conversation partner, across all Connectors, to a person in its memory.

## Who the Klex Bot is

The soul defines the Klex Bot's character and identity — its nature, rather than the memories and skills it picks up along the way. It is written by the Klex Bot itself and can only be changed through god messages in the god session, so no ordinary conversation can rewrite who the Klex Bot is. See [The soul of a Klex Bot](/bot/concepts/soul) for what it contains and how it is created and updated.
