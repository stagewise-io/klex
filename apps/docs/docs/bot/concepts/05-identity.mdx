A Klex Bot has a name and a [soul](/bot/concepts/soul), which describes its character and priorities. Its connected accounts determine how it appears and what it can access in external apps.

<Tabs syncKey="hosting" hash={false}>
<Tab title="Cloud-hosted">

In Klex Cloud, open your Bot and select **Edit bot** next to its name. The Bot must be connected, and you need organization owner or admin access to make changes. Change the **Name** field.

</Tab>
<Tab title="Self-hosted">

In the Klex app, **Settings → Bot Identity** shows the Bot’s official name and lets you update it.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

The Bot’s configured name is included in its session context.

## Identity in connected apps

For most connected apps, a Bot acts through its own account. Configure that account’s permissions in the app itself. See [Connections](/bot/concepts/brain-and-computers) for how to connect environments.
