A Klex Bot has a name and a [soul](/bot/concepts/soul), which describes its character and priorities. Its connected accounts determine how it appears and what it can access in external apps.

In the self-hosted Klex 0.30.1 app, **Settings → Bot Identity** shows the Bot’s official name and lets you update it. The Bot’s configured name is included in its session context.

## Identity in connected apps

For most connected apps, a Bot acts through its own account. Configure that account’s permissions in the app itself. See [Connections](/bot/concepts/brain-and-computers) for how to connect environments.
