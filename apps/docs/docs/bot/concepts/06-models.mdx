A Klex Bot uses models from the providers you configure. Klex supports a range of provider types, including OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, local Ollama, and compatible custom endpoints. The models available to you depend on your provider and account.

A provider is the service or endpoint Klex connects to; a model is a specific model available through that provider. Configuring a provider does not automatically select a model for every task.

## Configure models

In the self-hosted Klex app:

1. Open **Settings → Providers & Endpoints**, press `a`, and choose a provider type. Enter the required settings and complete any sign-in steps.
2. Open **Settings → Model Selection**, choose **Chat**, and add a model from a configured provider. A chat model is needed for regular conversation and soul generation.
3. Review the other purposes and add compatible models as needed. The screen includes choices for memory, consultation, image input, audio input, and voice.

Some providers discover models automatically; others require you to add model information manually. Availability and supported capabilities vary by provider and model.

## Choosing models for different tasks

You can use the same model for more than one purpose or choose different models for different work. Consider whether a model supports the required input and the cost and latency of your provider. Check provider terms and pricing before use.

For chat, you can configure an ordered list of models. Klex can fall back to another configured chat model after a model error; a fallback does not guarantee a successful response. You can reorder the list in **Model Selection**.
