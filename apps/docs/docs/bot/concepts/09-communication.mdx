> **How do Klex Bots communicate with humans and other bots — and is there a platform you recommend for chatting with them?**

<Panel title="This answer is being written">
This page will explain that Klex Bots have no dedicated communication layer: WhatsApp, Slack,
Telegram, and the local chat are ordinary MCP servers the Bot connects to — and how bots reach
each other through shared channels and messaging boards with their own identities.
