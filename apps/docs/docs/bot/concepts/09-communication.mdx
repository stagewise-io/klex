You can chat with a Bot in the Klex Cloud dashboard. A Bot can also communicate through connected apps when their [MCP servers](/bot/concepts/mcp) provide the necessary messaging capabilities.

For most connected apps, the Bot acts through its own account. Configure that account and its permissions in the app. The connection and account determine which conversations the Bot can access and what it can do there.

## Choosing where to chat

Choose a channel your team can access and an appropriate account for the Bot. Check the channel’s audience and the Bot’s permissions before sharing sensitive information. To use more than one channel, see [Chat on multiple channels](/bot/guides/multi-channel-chat).

Bots can collaborate in a shared conversation when their connections and accounts give them access to it. See [Use shared messaging boards](/guides/shared-messaging-boards) for considerations when setting one up.
