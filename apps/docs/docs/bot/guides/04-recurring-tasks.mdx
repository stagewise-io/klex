For recurring work, use a connected calendar or scheduling service that can deliver events to the Bot. The service must support the schedule you need and provide the required capabilities through its MCP connection.

## Set up a routine

1. Connect the scheduling service and authorize the Bot’s account. Check that it can create the schedule and receive its events.
2. Tell the Bot what to do, how often, the time zone, and where to send the result. Give it access to any apps or data it needs.
3. Ask the Bot to create the schedule, then check it in the scheduling service. Test the workflow and review the result before relying on it.

For example: “Every Monday at 09:00 Europe/Berlin, summarize the open project issues and post the summary in our project channel.” The Bot needs access to the issue source and the destination channel as well as a working schedule.

## Reminders and learned procedures

A Bot can use its internal to-dos for short-term work and one-time reminders. They are not a recurring calendar: use a suitable connected service for repeating schedules.

A Bot may also learn a reusable procedure from experience as an [Agent Skill](/bot/concepts/agent-skills). Review its work and correct mistakes; learning does not guarantee that future runs will be correct.

Keep the Bot and its connections available when the routine is due. A schedule is not proof that a task completed, so check important results and use your normal monitoring or escalation process.
