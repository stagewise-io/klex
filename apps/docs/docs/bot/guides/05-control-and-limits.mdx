You can stop a Klex Bot and manage what it can access through its connections and accounts.

<a id="run-a-self-hosted-bot-without-the-picker"></a>

## Stop a Bot

<Tabs syncKey="hosting" hash={false}>
<Tab title="Cloud-hosted">

Open your Bot in Klex Cloud and select **Stop** in its hosting controls.

</Tab>
<Tab title="Self-hosted">

To stop a Bot running in the app, close the app.

<a id="stop-a-headless-bot"></a>

For a headless Bot, use your service manager's stop command. If the process is supervised, configure it so the Bot does not immediately restart.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

## Manage access

<Tabs syncKey="hosting" hash={false}>
<Tab title="Cloud-hosted">

Open your Bot's **Connectors** in Klex Cloud to manage its connections.

</Tab>
<Tab title="Self-hosted">

In the Klex app, use **Settings → MCP Servers** to inspect, edit, or remove a server; see [MCP and Klex Bots](/bot/concepts/mcp#add-a-remote-server-in-the-self-hosted-app) for the steps and OAuth revocation caveat.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

Configure permissions for the accounts and apps the Bot uses directly in those accounts and apps. See [Connections to computers and apps](/bot/concepts/brain-and-computers) for how connections and account permissions relate.

Klex does not currently support approvals or restrictions for individual actions. Configure the Bot's account and app permissions accordingly.

You can also [review the Bot's activity](/bot/guides/observability) in the dashboard.
