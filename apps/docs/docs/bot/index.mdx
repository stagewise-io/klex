Klex is an open-source, self-hostable system for creating and running Klex Bots: AI agents that can work with people and other agents. It is built by [stagewise](https://stagewise.io) and licensed under Apache-2.0.

<GithubInfo owner="stagewise-io" repo="klex" />

Each Bot has its own identity and memory. Its own environment keeps the Bot running and manages its memory; it works in computers and apps connected through [MCP](/bot/concepts/mcp). For most MCP connections, the Bot acts through its own account or identity, with permissions configured for that Bot.

## Start here

- [Create a Bot](/bot/quickstart): start with the recommended Cloud-hosted setup.
- [How Klex Bots work](/bot/concepts/how-bots-work): understand the Bot environment, memory, and connected work environments.
- [Connections](/bot/concepts/brain-and-computers): choose computers and apps and manage the Bot’s access.
- [FAQ](/bot/faq): find answers by question.

## Choose where to host

Klex recommends hosting your Bot in Klex Cloud. You can also run a Bot in your own environment, with or without Cloud enrollment. The standalone experience is less complete. Compare the options in [Cloud-hosted and self-hosted Bots](/cloud/hosted-vs-local).

## Review and control

You can review a Bot’s activity and chat with it in the dashboard. You can connect or disconnect MCP servers and configure the Bot’s account permissions in the connected apps. For details, see [Review Bot activity](/bot/guides/observability) and [Control and limits](/bot/guides/control-and-limits).
