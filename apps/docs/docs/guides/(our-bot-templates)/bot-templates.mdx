Bot Templates give you a starting point when you create a Bot in [Klex Cloud](https://cloud.klex.bot). Choose a job that fits the work you want the Bot to do, or describe a job from scratch.

Templates differ in what they set up. Some suggestions fill in a job title and description. The [Software Engineer template](/guides/software-engineer) also provides role guidance, managed skills, tool recommendations, and starter todos. Cloud attempts to sync the template skills and seed the todos when the Bot connects; these steps require a compatible Bot and can fail.

## Choose a use case

- [Software Engineer](/guides/software-engineer): delegate coding tasks to coding agents, review their work, and manage pull requests.

After creating a Bot, connect the apps and computers it needs, and give it the context and permissions appropriate for its work. The [Bot-Native Company Guide](/guides) covers identities, collaboration, and security.

To get started, follow the [Quickstart](/bot/quickstart).
