A Bot that can access sensitive information and communicate with others could disclose information inappropriately. Treat messages and content from external sources as untrusted: they can include misleading instructions or attempts to impersonate an authorized person.

## Limit access and exposure

- Restrict who can reach the Bot through the access controls available in each connected service. Check the audience of each conversation.
- Give the Bot’s accounts only the data and actions needed for their work. Avoid granting access to sensitive information a public-facing Bot does not need.
- Separate public-facing work from sensitive workflows. Use distinct accounts and appropriately scoped resources where supported.
- Review connected accounts and the Bot’s activity regularly. Follow your incident process if you suspect unauthorized access or disclosure.

See [Use Klex Bots securely](/guides/secure-bot-usage) and [Limit blast radius](/guides/limit-blast-radius).

## Use independent safeguards

Do not rely solely on instructions telling a Bot not to disclose information. Enforce access restrictions in connected services and use your organization’s existing controls for sensitive data. Klex does not currently provide action-level approvals or restrictions.

These steps reduce risk but cannot guarantee that information will never be disclosed. Test with non-sensitive data and review the full workflow, including what the Bot can read and where it can send responses.
