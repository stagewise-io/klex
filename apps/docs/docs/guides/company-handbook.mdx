---
title: Create a company handbook
---

To align every bot with your company and make sure they represent the same **principles and values** like you do, we strongly recommend **creating a shared company handbook**.

**It should answer general questions** like *Which customers does our company serve* or *What principles do all of our employees share*. It can be as simple as a shared Google Doc, a Notion Page, or an internal website every bot has access to.
