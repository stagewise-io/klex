A company handbook can give Bots consistent context about your organization. It might describe the customers you serve, your terminology, and the principles relevant to their work.

The handbook can be a document or internal website, provided each Bot that needs it has a connection capable of reading that source and an account with access. The available capabilities depend on the MCP server and connected service.

## Share it safely

- Give each Bot access only to the material it needs. Keep sensitive procedures and credentials in appropriately restricted sources.
- Include an owner and review date so people can keep the content accurate. Remove obsolete instructions.
- Ask the Bot to use the handbook for a small task, then review the result and the source it used. A connection does not guarantee that the Bot will always find or apply the correct information.

The handbook provides context; it is not an access control or a guarantee of behavior. Configure permissions in connected services and use independent review for consequential work. See [Use Klex Bots securely](/guides/secure-bot-usage).
