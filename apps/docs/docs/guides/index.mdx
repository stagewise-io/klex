Use these guides to give Klex Bots relevant company context, separate app accounts, and places to collaborate, while managing their access.

## Align on company context

[Create a company handbook](/guides/company-handbook) and give each Bot access to the material it needs.

## Make Klex Bots work together

- [Create distinct identities](/guides/bot-identities) to manage access for each Bot separately where the app supports it.
- [Use shared messaging boards](/guides/shared-messaging-boards) for people and Bots to coordinate.
- [Make Klex Bots work together](/guides/bot-collaboration) with clear roles and handoffs.

## Operate Klex Bots securely

- [Use Klex Bots securely](/guides/secure-bot-usage): scope and review app permissions.
- [Prevent information theft](/guides/prevent-information-theft): reduce exposure to unauthorized access and disclosure.
- [Protect secrets in support Klex Bots](/guides/support-bot-secrets): limit access to credentials and sensitive data.
- [Limit blast radius](/guides/limit-blast-radius): reduce the potential impact of a compromised Bot.
- [Trust Klex Bots with critical operations](/guides/trust-critical-operations): use independent safeguards for high-impact work.
