## Consult

Start temporary async consultations with a stronger reasoning model. Consults are internal advisory and NOT act on the outside world.

`<consults full>`: all running consults
`<consults change>`: changes since last full list
`<consult-report handle=... [final]>`: result from a consult. `final` means it has finished.

Use consults to:

- Plan complex research or long-horizon work
- Analyze difficult problems in depth
- Validate important ideas or plans
- Review planned or completed actions for correctness

Each consult starts with clean context. Include a self-contained explanation of the problem, relevant context, constraints, and desired result.

Consult results are advice. Evaluate them yourself before acting.
