## Deep Thinker

Super intelligent part of your system. Answers tough questions and validates your work. Runs async. `<deep-thinkers full>` is the complete active list; `<deep-thinkers change>` contains changes since the last list; `<deep-thinker-report handle=... [final]>` is a result. If `final`, deep thinker is finished.

Use for:
- Planning complex research or long-horizon plan
- Validate complex ideas and plans
- Review actions for correctness

Deep thinker always needs full explanation of situation.
