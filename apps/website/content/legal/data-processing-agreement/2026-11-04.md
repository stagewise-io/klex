## 1. Parties and scope

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the Klex Cloud agreement ("Agreement") between **stagewise GmbH**, Obernstraße 50, 33602 Bielefeld, Germany ("stagewise", "we"), and the business customer identified for the relevant Klex Cloud organization ("Customer", "you"). It takes effect when incorporated into that organization's Agreement under the [Klex Cloud Terms of Service](/legal/terms/2026-11-04). No separate signature or DPA-specific acceptance is required. Each organization's Agreement remains separate. The DPA version incorporated at acceptance applies; publication of a newer version does not itself amend an existing Agreement.

This DPA applies to personal data we process on your behalf to provide Klex Cloud, including hosted Klex Bots, Klex Machines, APIs, integrations and related support ("Customer Personal Data"). For self-hosted Bots, it covers only processing we actually perform through Cloud or agreed support, not infrastructure you operate independently. It does not cover stagewise IDE.

You act as controller or, where you process data for another controller, as its authorized processor. We act as your processor or subprocessor accordingly. You must have the relevant controller's authority to engage us and issue instructions.

GDPR terms have the meanings in Regulation (EU) 2016/679. Applicable EU and Member State data-protection law governs processing under this DPA; other mandatory law remains unaffected. A "Subprocessor" is a processor we engage to carry out part of this processing. A "Personal Data Breach" has the meaning in Article 4(12) GDPR.

Our independent processing for purposes such as our own account administration, billing and separately defined analytics is described in the Privacy Policy, not authorized by this DPA. Actual purposes determine the role: instructed content copied into support or diagnostic systems remains protected under this DPA. Controller-side retention does not authorize unrelated retention or reuse of that content.

Contact **privacy@klex.bot** for data-protection matters and **support@klex.bot** for service, return or deletion requests.

## 2. Processing and instructions

Annex 1 describes the processing. You determine its purposes and are responsible for a lawful basis, appropriate notices, permissions and lawful instructions. You configure users, workloads and connected-system permissions appropriate to your use. These responsibilities do not excuse our own obligations, including enforcement of agreed access and approval restrictions.

We process Customer Personal Data only on documented instructions, including the Agreement, this DPA, configurations and actions of authorized users, and additional documented instructions. Bot actions within an authorized workload do not authorize independent use by stagewise.

If Union or Member State law requires other processing, we inform you of the requirement before processing unless the law prohibits this on important public-interest grounds. We immediately inform you if, in our opinion, an instruction infringes the GDPR or other Union or Member State data-protection provisions. The parties address the concern before the affected processing continues, except where lawful preservation or other mandatory action is required.

We do not use Customer Content to train our models, independently exploit Bot content or memory, or conduct unrelated product research. Necessary hosting, execution, storage, transformation and routing to deliver the instructed service remain permitted. Customer-agreed troubleshooting does not authorize training or unrelated use by us or our Subprocessors.

Additional instructions outside the agreed service scope may require agreement on implementation. No new assistance or audit fee is created by this DPA, and commercial arrangements must not obstruct mandatory obligations.

## 3. Confidentiality, security and debugging

We ensure that persons authorized to process Customer Personal Data are bound by confidentiality or an appropriate statutory obligation. Access is limited to authorized responsibilities and the information needed for them. Confidentiality duties continue after access or the Agreement ends.

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures under Article 32 GDPR, taking account of the state of the art, implementation costs, the processing and risks to individuals. Annex 2 describes the agreed measures. We assess their effectiveness regularly and may update them without materially reducing overall protection or overriding an expressly agreed obligation.

Full-content Bot debug tracing is off by default. Only technical staff enable it, following discussion and agreement with you, for a limited time and the agreed purpose. Such access is audit-logged. Traces can contain complete conversations, sensitive information and LLM inputs and outputs. They are retained for no more than 30 days and can be deleted earlier on request. Other operational telemetry follows its separately applicable purpose and retention; it must not be used to bypass these limits for full-content traces.

Temporary support debugging may involve necessary excerpts in the support and issue-management tools in Annex 3. Our own Bots may analyze agreed debug traces using the troubleshooting inference providers listed there. The same agreement, purpose limits, access logging, Subprocessor obligations and transfer protections apply. Information shared is limited to what is necessary for the investigation.

API keys and integration credentials are not intentionally included in logs or traces. This is not a guarantee that accidental inclusion can never occur or that all sensitive content is automatically redacted. Necessary security or legally required processing must have its own lawful justification and is not permission for unrelated use.

## 4. Model providers and connections

Runtime model access may use a provider account/API key you supply or be included in an expressly agreed Cloud offering. Unless such an offering includes model access, you supply the necessary access under your own provider arrangement. This DPA does not itself include model access or guarantee any particular provider's availability. You may also connect messaging channels, MCP services and other tools. Relevant content, credentials, instructions and tool inputs/outputs are transmitted as needed for the authorized connection. Transformation of content does not necessarily make it anonymous.

A provider's role depends on the actual arrangement, not simply who pays or supplies an API key. Providers we engage to perform our processing obligations are subject to section 5. A provider you engage independently operates under your applicable arrangement; this does not remove our duties for processing and transmission we perform. You must ensure your instructions and connections are lawful and suitable for the workload.

No promise is made that every independently selected provider has the same training, retention or location settings. This section does not reclassify stagewise-engaged troubleshooting providers as your independent providers.

## 5. Subprocessors

The Customer grants general written authorization for the Subprocessors and processing functions identified in our publicly available [Subprocessor List](https://trust.stagewise.io), which is incorporated into this DPA by reference. The list identifies the Subprocessors authorized at the time the Agreement takes effect. Subsequent additions or replacements are governed by the notification and objection procedure in this section.

Before adding or replacing a Subprocessor, we notify your designated contract or privacy contact by email of its identity, relevant function, processing locations and transfer safeguards sufficiently in advance to give you a reasonable opportunity to object on data-protection grounds. Updating the public list does not independently authorize an addition or replacement without following this procedure.

We work with you to resolve a timely objection before the disputed processing begins, including considering an alternative arrangement. If no reasonable solution is available, the parties address discontinuation or termination of the affected service under the Agreement and mandatory law before the disputed processing begins. This process does not permit unilateral changes to unrelated contractual rights.

We bind each Subprocessor by a written contract imposing the applicable data-protection obligations required by Article 28(4) GDPR, including sufficient security guarantees. We remain fully liable to you for performance of those obligations as required by that Article. Necessary information about these arrangements is available on request, subject to lawful confidentiality protections.

Your use of the Services does not transfer your own controller obligations to us or remove our processor obligations.

## 8. Personal Data Breaches

We notify affected Customers directly by email without undue delay after becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach affecting their Customer Personal Data. Notice is sent to an appropriate designated contract, privacy or security contact, or an authorized organization owner where no separate contact has been designated. Please keep contact details current; we take reasonable steps to reach an authorized contact where needed.

To the extent available, notice describes the nature of the breach, affected categories and approximate numbers of individuals and records, likely consequences, response and mitigation measures, and a contact for further information. Missing information may follow in stages without undue further delay. Notification is not postponed until an investigation is complete.

Our DevOps personnel coordinate response, containment and remediation, preserve relevant evidence and cooperate with you. Public status updates may supplement but do not replace direct notice. You determine notifications you must make to individuals and authorities, without restricting legal duties independently imposed on us. No fixed hourly notification or resolution guarantee is created, and notice is not itself an admission of liability.

## 10. Return, deletion and residual copies

At your choice, we return or delete Customer Personal Data after the processing services end and delete existing copies unless Union or Member State law requires storage. We also comply with valid deletion instructions during the Agreement. Return, switching and retrieval rights under the Agreement and mandatory law remain preserved.

Contact support@klex.bot to arrange secure return or deletion without requiring continued account access. We may verify authority proportionately. Return scope, formats and any applicable switching timelines follow the relevant service arrangement and mandatory law. Any remaining retrieval requirement is coordinated with your instructions before deletion is initiated, not used to postpone an already triggered deletion maximum.

**When a Bot or Machine is deleted, its Customer Personal Data is deleted within a maximum of 30 days after that resource's deletion. When an organization is deleted, its data and all its Bots' data are deleted within a maximum of 30 days after organization deletion.** If organization deletion requires managed Machines to be deleted first, each Machine remains subject to its own 30-day maximum measured from its deletion.

These maxima include backups and copies held in Grafana, support/debugging systems and other systems within stagewise's processing chain, including relevant Subprocessor copies. Copies awaiting deletion remain protected and restricted to the necessary recovery, protection or deletion process. If a backup is restored, applicable deletion instructions are reapplied before affected data returns to ordinary use. Earlier action is taken where law requires it.

These commitments do not purport to erase copies independently held by providers outside our processing chain under your separate arrangements. Deleting a user account does not by itself instruct deletion of shared organization data. Stopping execution, pausing a resource or exhausting credits is not the same as deleting it. A credit-related minimum period before discretionary deletion is not a deletion SLA or permission for indefinite retention.

Customer Personal Data that Union or Member State law requires us to retain is appropriately restricted to that purpose, protected under this DPA and deleted when the requirement ends. Independent controller-side financial or legal recordkeeping does not justify retaining an entire live workload. We confirm completion of instructed deletion on request.

Amendments follow the Agreement's amendment provisions and applicable law. Publication, silence or continued use alone is not deemed acceptance of changes to contractual rights. ## Annex 2 — Technical and organizational measures

- **Encryption and credentials.** Managed production customer-data paths and storage use encryption in transit and at rest, including databases, files, Bot/Machine disks, backups and applicable Grafana traces. Customer API keys and integration credentials are stored on each Bot's encrypted AWS EBS hosting volume. No separate application-level encryption is implied. These descriptions do not promise encryption for arbitrary Customer-configured endpoints or independently self-hosted infrastructure.
- **Personnel access.** Production customer-data access requires MFA, Cloudflare Zero Trust, explicit allowlisting and sufficient permissions. Access is recorded in infrastructure and/or product audit logs. Authorized personnel are subject to confidentiality and purpose restrictions.
- **Isolation.** Each Bot runs in an independent container; each Machine is isolated at KVM virtual-machine level. Application permissions enforce user, organization and Bot access boundaries. Containers and virtual machines are distinct isolation measures, not identical guarantees.
- **Vulnerability management.** GitHub Dependabot and regular internal checks support vulnerability identification. Fixes are deployed promptly to managed infrastructure, and open-source Bot updates are made available for customer-managed deployments. Customers manage updates to their independently self-hosted software. No fixed remediation deadline is promised.
- **Recovery.** Daily backups are taken for managed Bot and Machine data, and restoration is tested periodically. No fixed restoration-test frequency, recovery-point or recovery-time guarantee is promised. Backup deletion remains subject to section 10.
- **Debug controls.** Full-content tracing is off by default, staff-enabled only after Customer agreement, time-limited, purpose-restricted and audit-logged, with 30-day retention and earlier deletion available. Credentials are not intentionally logged; comprehensive automatic redaction or impossibility of accidental inclusion is not promised.
- **Incidents and review.** DevOps personnel coordinate incident handling and direct notification under section 8. Measures are assessed regularly for effectiveness, including through vulnerability checks and periodic restoration testing. No unagreed security certification is implied.
