## 2. Automatic top-up authorization

Automatic top-up is optional. Its authorization identifies the payment method, balance threshold, purchase amount and maximum number of automatic payment attempts per UTC calendar month. Saving a payment method alone does not authorize top-ups. You may revoke future top-ups through the available billing controls or by contacting support. Revocation does not reverse a payment already submitted, stop resources or terminate the organization's agreement.

Under the current attempt limit, each new automatic payment attempt counts, including pending, failed, cancelled and action-required payments. You may reach the limit without receiving credits. Continuing the same pending payment does not count again or authorize duplicate charging. This is an attempt limit, not a spending cap or a limit on resource usage or manual purchases. A change to what you authorize requires a valid revised authorization; a documentation update alone is insufficient.

Purchased balance is prepaid value for the services you authorize. Purchased credits do not expire under these conditions. Rights attached to earlier purchases remain unaffected. Any expiry condition for future purchases must be lawful and clearly agreed before purchase; these conditions do not impose one.

Promotional credits have no cash-redemption value and may have expiry or revocation conditions disclosed when granted. Those conditions do not retroactively charge for covered usage or diminish purchased value. The current consumption order is promotional credits, remedy credits, then purchased or restored purchased balance. Replenishment does not itself authorize additional resources.

Credits restoring purchased balance or satisfying a remedy retain the rights attached to the underlying entitlement. Promotional-credit rules do not reduce those rights. We do not substitute credits for an owed cash repayment without lawful agreement. Settlement of unused purchased value on termination follows the Terms.

## 4. Stopping, resuming and retention

Insufficient balance or a payment restriction may prevent paid execution. Replenishing balance or resolving a review does not guarantee immediate resumption. Follow the resource controls and status information; the current service requires an authorized User to resume stopped resources. We do not impose new paid activity merely because funds become available.

Stopping execution, ending a resource commitment, deleting a resource and terminating the organization's agreement are distinct. We may delete retained resource data because of an exhausted balance only after the corresponding service balance has remained without available credits for at least 30 consecutive days. Deletion after that point is discretionary, not an automatic schedule or a promise of continued retention. A longer agreed retention period remains binding. This minimum does not prevent a deletion you request or processing required by law, and it does not guarantee that a resource remains executable. Other deletion grounds remain subject to the Terms and law.

These conditions introduce no storage fee and do not promise indefinite or free inactive storage.
